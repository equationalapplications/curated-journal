Curated Journal — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026 · Applies to the Curated Journal applications for iOS and Android.

Terms of Service for this app are published at …/curated-journal/terms.

The short version

No account. No analytics. No crash reporting. No advertising. No telemetry. Your journal is stored on your phone and is never transmitted to us — we operate no server that receives it, and we could not read it if we wanted to.

Who we are

Curated Journal is published by Equational Applications LLC ("EA," "we"), a Michigan limited liability company. Contact: info@equationalapplications.com.

What stays on your device

  • Your journal — entries, the notes and connections the AI derives from them, citations, and the knowledge graph — is stored in a local database (SQLite) on your device.
  • AI model files you install are stored on your device.
  • Local preferences (a random database identifier, your chosen model's path and name) are stored in your device's secure storage. The identifier is random, carries no personal information, and never leaves the device.

None of this is synced, uploaded, or backed up to us. There is no cloud copy.

On-device AI

The AI model runs entirely on your device. Your entries and everything the app derives from them are processed locally and are not sent to EA or to any AI provider. EA operates no models and no inference servers.

The only network activity — and you start each one

The app makes no network connections on its own. Automatic checks are disabled by configuration. Three things happen only when you explicitly start them:

  • Model downloads. When you install a model in the model hub, the model file is fetched from the third-party host that publishes it (currently Hugging Face). The request is an ordinary file download; we add no identifiers to it. That host's own privacy policy governs what it collects — see Hugging Face's privacy policy. You can also import a model file (.gguf) you obtained yourself.
  • Update checks. When you run the update check in Settings, the app contacts EA's update-delivery provider (Expo) to see whether a new app version is available. No journal content is involved. The app never checks automatically at launch.
  • Voluntary tips. If you choose to leave a tip, the purchase is processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). EA never receives your payment details or personal information from the transaction; the store's own privacy policy applies to it.

Everything else — writing, organizing, asking, exploring, exporting — works with no network at all, including in airplane mode.

What we never do

  • We do not collect analytics, usage statistics, or crash reports.
  • We do not advertise, track, or profile you.
  • We do not sell or share personal information — we do not have any to sell.
  • We do not require an account, and we never ask for your name, email, or contacts inside the app.

Export and deletion

You can export your entire knowledge base at any time in the open OKF format, saved through your device's own file picker to wherever you choose. Because there is no server copy, deleting the app and its data from your device is complete and final — it is removed everywhere. We cannot recover, reset, or restore your journal; please export before uninstalling.

Children

Curated Journal is not directed at children and is rated for adults (18+). Use is subject to the eligibility terms in the app's Terms of Service.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the revised version here with a new effective date, and the app's about screen will point to the current version. The app's network behavior only ever changes through app updates, which you choose to install.
